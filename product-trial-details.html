Throw an elite AI experience challenge on campus! Participants will interact with Gemini Chat to engineer creative party schedules, custom welcome logs, and interactive campus games. ⏱️ 45-60 Mins 📂 Google Doc Setup 👥 10-50 Attendees

Step-by-Step Task Roadmap

1
Pre-Survey Form: Fill out the initial registration survey and make sure to include your ambassador's tracking information.
2
Gemini Blueprint Build: Launch Gemini Chat, pitch your core party concept, and let it map out detailed game workflows, icebreakers, and timelines.
3
Stress-Test Verification: Ask Gemini to find the weakest parts of your event structure. Fix the gaps and finalize your complete text content inside a clean Google Doc.
4
Nano Banana Canvas: Input your descriptive party environment brief into Nano Banana to generate a highly vivid, detailed invitation poster.
5
60-Second Room Pitch: Present your complete event poster and plan to the room for an immediate "Would Attend" or "Would Skip" live vote.
6
Final Submission: Upload your structured Google Document link, your Nano Banana canvas graphic, and the shareable Gemini chat log history.
